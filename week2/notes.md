CS 50- Week 2: Arrays


Notes and reflections for Week 2

This week of CS50 focused on working with arrays and strings in C, where I built programs to analyse and manipulate text. I developed a readability calculator using the Coleman-Liau index, which determines the reading level of a given text by counting letters, words and sentences, and also implemented a Caesar cipher that encrypts text based on a user-provided key. Through these tasks, I strengthened my understanding of how strings work in C, how to loop through and process character data, and how to apply logic using functions like isalpha, isdigit, and ASCII values. One of the biggest challenges for me was dealing with small syntax issues and structuring my code correctly, especially when working with functions and command-line arguments, as minor mistakes would often break the whole program. However, by compiling frequently, debugging properly, and rewriting code to fully understand it, I improved my attention to detail and problem-solving approach. Overall, this week pushed me to think more like a developer rather than just writing code, and gave me a solid foundation for handling more complex logic going forward.
